# Research Logbook

This tracked logbook carries concise findings forward between agent attempts.
Agents read it before proposing changes. Sentinel appends a summary after
each completed evaluation. Raw hypothesis notes, candidate workspaces, and full
logs remain in the gitignored experimentation or rejected directories.

## Guidance for agents

- Tie every conclusion to its candidate, evaluator, metric, and configuration.
- Treat results as evidence for the tested conditions, not universal truth.
- A rejected hypothesis can be revisited when the candidate, context, or test
  conditions materially change; explain the difference.
- Prefer one interpretable hypothesis per attempt and disclose confounding
  changes.
- Do not edit this logbook during an attempt. Return findings to Sentinel.
